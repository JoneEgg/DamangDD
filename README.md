RULES AND CONTROLS =====
- Avoid insects, other spiders, lizards, and crumbling platforms by jumping over or under them (space)
- Collect food to fill up your health bar and rack up scores
- Losing one life makes you impervious to all damage for a few seconds
- Losing all your lives is a Game Over
- One your health bar is full, use your right mouse button to gain an extra life
